# E07 Stage A

The June 3 target is an optional invitation to an internal pitch-comp budget review. Its operative text is: "You're welcome to join if you want visibility but it's not necessary." It provides no day, time, deadline, request to arrange the meeting, or evidence Mark has elected to attend. Under the assistant instructions, which warn against over-acting and direct no changes for messages needing none, leaving the calendar and to-dos unchanged is supported.

There is no supported exact date or scheduling window for this optional review. The default 9 a.m./one-hour event rules do not supply a missing calendar date or turn optional attendance into an obligation.

A June 1 message from Finance separately requested a meeting with Mark and VP Product on Sunday, July 5, 2026, to discuss prize money and event costs. That is an affirmative scheduling request with an explicit date. The target does not explicitly revise, cancel, confirm, or identify itself as that meeting. Shared pitch-comp budget subject matter alone is insufficient to merge their identities or change the earlier meeting.

The other earlier invitations are optional, and the same-day HR message explicitly needs nothing. Neither creates a target-email obligation. Uncertainty remains whether the optional review is related to the July 5 meeting, but the available wording authorizes no replacement, deletion, new event, or task.
